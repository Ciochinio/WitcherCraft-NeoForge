Pause Menu Creator

Edits the WitcherCraft pause menu - a centred group of tabs that works just like the shell navbar, only bigger and over its own background. Each tab opens the shell on its pageId. The level/XP readout is shared with the shell and edited in gui-layout-creator.html (shown here as a greyed reference). Output regenerates PauseMenuLayout.java → Copy → paste over the file.

A tab's pageId must match a shell GuiPage.id() - clicking it opens that shell tab. Same ids as the navbar (glossary, inventory, map, alchemy, skills, meditation).
Design canvas & navbar
Keep Design W:H at 16:9 (e.g. 640x360) - the background should be authored at that same ratio, or it stretches within the canvas. The tabs are a centred group at Nav Y.
